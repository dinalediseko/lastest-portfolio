4M YPD Convention 2024

Event branding, creative direction, and campaign design for the Mangena Maake Mokone Memorial Conference 2024 Convention

About the Project

This was the visual identity and event campaign created for the 2024 M.M. Mokone Memorial Conference YPD Mission MPossible: Breaking Camp Convention under the African Methodist Episcopal Church (19th Episcopal District). The visual direction was built to capture the urgency, movement, and spiritual momentum behind the convention theme, while still feeling youthful, energetic, and rooted in community.

I led the creative direction for this campaign from concept to execution, designing the event logo, hero poster, packing list collateral, and supporting visual assets. I also hand-drew the doodle elements used throughout the design system to give the campaign a more personal and expressive feel, while also intentionally referencing the nature of Convention itself as a reporting space — a moment of reflection, review, and accountability around the objectives set at the beginning of the year. The sketch-like visual language was therefore used not only as a stylistic choice, but as a conceptual nod to planning notes, annotations, strategy sessions, and progress mapping. To further root the campaign in the life of the ministry, I incorporated photography I personally captured at a previous youth event, allowing the visuals to feel lived-in, familiar, and authentic to the audience it was created for.

Beyond aesthetics, the goal was clear communication: to create visuals that not only felt compelling, but also helped attendees quickly understand the event details, venue information, dress expectations, and preparation requirements. The final outcome balanced branding, storytelling, information design, and ministry communication — a reflection of the kind of work I love most: design that carries both message and meaning.

“This project reflects the kind of work I do in service spaces — building visuals that don’t just look good, but help people feel invited, informed, and part of something bigger.”
- D’SEIKOU
