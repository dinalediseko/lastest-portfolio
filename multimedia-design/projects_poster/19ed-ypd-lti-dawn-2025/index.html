4M Celebrates Nelson Mandela Day

A tribute poster celebrating Nelson Mandela Day through layered imagery, bold type, and intentional visual storytelling.

About the Project

4M Celebrates Nelson Mandela Day is a commemorative poster created to honour the life and legacy of Nelson Mandela in a way that felt visually bold, reflective, and culturally grounded. I wanted the piece to feel more than informational — something that could also function as a tribute and visual moment of remembrance.

The design brings together bold typography, layered imagery, and an editorial-inspired collage approach to capture Mandela’s presence, impact, and symbolism across generations. Through scale, composition, and contrast, I aimed to create a piece that felt both powerful and intentional, while still remaining accessible and engaging for a younger audience.

At its core, this poster was about using design to hold memory and meaning — not just to acknowledge a date on the calendar, but to visually reflect on leadership, legacy, and the kind of greatness younger generations are still being called into.

“This project reflects the kind of work I do in service spaces — building visuals that don’t just look good, but help people feel invited, informed, and part of something bigger.”
- D’SEIKOU
