Design Project Details

Mokone’s
Very Own

Custom appreciation jackets designed to honour leadership, legacy, and service within the Mangena Maake Mokone Memorial YPD Conference.

About the Project

Mokone’s Very Own was a commemorative jacket design project created as an appreciation gift for outgoing presidents of the Mangena Maake Mokone Memorial Conference YPD, in recognition of their leadership, service, and contribution to the organisation. The intention behind the project was to create something that felt both personal and prestigious — not just merchandise, but a wearable keepsake that honoured their legacy of service.

I was responsible for both the creative design and the project management of the jackets, from developing the visual direction and symbolic structure of the garment, to sourcing a supplier and managing the production process through to execution. Each jacket was intentionally designed as a personalised recognition piece, with every element carrying meaning tied to the individual’s term of office and identity within the organisation.

The final composition of the jacket included the year in which their service concluded on one sleeve, their initials on the opposite sleeve, a featured personal quote on the chest and back, and a Roman numeral indicating the number president they were in the organisation’s history. In this case, for example, R.B.S. Mashishi was recognised as the 18th elected president, while Z.O.E. Mnguni represented the 19th. The “Mokone’s Very Own” mark was developed to reinforce a sense of belonging, distinction, and institutional pride.

This project sits at the intersection of apparel design, symbolism, tribute design, and production coordination, reflecting my ability to not only create meaningful visual systems, but also carry a concept through the practical realities of execution and delivery.

“ This project reflects the kind of work I enjoy most — design that holds meaning, tells a story, and becomes something people can physically keep as part of their journey.”
- D’SEIKOU
