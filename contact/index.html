Quick FAQ

Short answers to help clients understand how working together usually works.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes. A 50% deposit is required to start most projects, with the remaining 50% due before final delivery.

How many revisions are included?

Most projects include 2 revision rounds. Extra revisions can be added if the project needs more iteration.

Can I request urgent work?

Yes, rush work can be discussed. Rush timelines may include an additional fee depending on urgency and scope.

Who owns the final files?

The client owns final deliverables after full payment. Source files can be discussed separately where applicable.

What about stock images, fonts or licences?

Any paid stock, fonts, subscriptions or licences needed for the project are usually billed to the client.

Are retainers available?

Yes. Retainers are available for ongoing design, content, website updates, reporting and project support.
